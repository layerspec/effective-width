1. r_max as the denominator, on every checkpoint
========================================================================
  A conv output cannot have rank above r_max = g*min((C_in/g)*kh*kw, C_out/g).
  If k* tracks r_max rather than C, then (i) k*(.999)/r_max never exceeds 1,
  (ii) k*(.999)/C at bounded layers scales with r_max/C while k*(.999)/r_max
  does not, and (iii) the bounded/unbounded difference vanishes once the
  denominator is r_max.

5. Where the bound applies: conv3 output vs block output (post-add ReLU)
========================================================================
  In a bottleneck block the 1x1 expansion conv3 has r_max = C/4 (C/2 for
  wide/ResNeXt), so k*(.999)/C <= 0.25 at its output by construction.
  The residual sum restores the rank: the same channels at the block
  output are not bounded.  This is why studies that hook block outputs
  (Ansuini 2019, Elmoznino & Bonner 2024) never saw the artefact, and why
  a per-layer protocol must state which tensor it measures.

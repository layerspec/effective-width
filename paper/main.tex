Our contributions are:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{A reconciliation.} We compute both families of metric on
  identical layers of identical models, under both the position-sampled and
  the globally-pooled estimator, and separate the four candidate explanations
  for the disagreement in the literature.
  \item \textbf{Coverage of the uncovered.} \cite{garg2019lowEffort}
  explicitly excluded residual networks; ConvNeXt \cite{liu2022convnext}
  postdates this literature entirely. We report both at ImageNet scale.
  \item \textbf{An initialisation control.} We measure the same profile in
  untrained networks, separating structure the network learned from structure
  implied by the architecture and the input statistics alone. To our knowledge
  no prior work reports this.
  \item \textbf{An explicit sample-size analysis.} We show what the estimator
  does when $n$ approaches $C$, give a convergence criterion for reporting, and
  \todo{state whether published curves are affected}.
\end{itemize}

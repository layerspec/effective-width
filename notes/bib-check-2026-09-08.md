## 1. bansal2018orthogonality

**一手資料**
- 官方 BibTeX（proceedings.neurips.cc）：title = "Can We Gain More from Orthogonality
  Regularizations in Training Deep Networks?"，booktitle = Advances in Neural Information
  Processing Systems，volume = 31，editor = S. Bengio and H. Wallach and H. Larochelle and
  K. Grauman and N. Cesa-Bianchi and R. Garnett，publisher = Curran Associates, Inc.，
  year = 2018，**pages = {}（空）**。
- **PDF 本身（NeurIPS 版與 arXiv 1810.09102v1 都一樣）印的標題是
  "Can We Gain More from Orthogonality Regularizations in Training Deep CNNs?"**。
  proceedings 索引與 PDF 標題不一致，是 NeurIPS 那邊的問題；GitHub 官方 repo 也用 CNNs。
- 作者：Nitin Bansal, Xiaohan Chen, Zhangyang Wang（Texas A&M）。一致。
- arXiv：1810.09102（v1，2018-10-22，"Accepted in NIPS 2018"）。

**refs.bib 差異**
- 缺 `volume = {31}`、`publisher`、`editor`、arXiv id。
- 頁碼：官方無；DBLP 應該有（記憶中是 4266–4276）但**這次查不到，不要照記憶填**。
- 標題：bib 用 proceedings 索引版 "Deep Networks?"，可以維持（與 NeurIPS 官方 BibTeX 一致），
  但要知道 PDF 是 "Deep CNNs?"；審稿人若拿 PDF 對，會看到不同標題。建議加 `note`。

**主張 (a)：SO/DSO/MC/SRIP 的定義；overcomplete W 的 Gram 矩陣秩虧損** —— **有，全部找到。**
- SO：§3.1（PDF 第 3 頁）Eq. (1)：`(SO) λ‖WᵀW − I‖²_F`
- DSO：§3.2（第 4 頁）Eq. (2)：`(DSO) λ(‖WᵀW − I‖²_F + ‖WWᵀ − I‖²_F)`
- MC：§3.3（第 4 頁）Eq. (4)：`(MC) λ‖WᵀW − I‖_∞`
- SRIP：§3.4（第 5 頁）Eq. (6)：`(SRIP) λ·σ(WᵀW − I)`，"It is termed as the Spectral
  Restricted Isometry Property (SRIP) regularization."
- 秩虧損，§3.1（第 4 頁）原句：
  > "However, SO (1) is flawed for an obvious reason: the columns of W could possibly be
  > mutually orthogonal, if and only if W is undercomplete (m ≥ n). For overcomplete W
  > (m < n), its gram matrix WᵀW ∈ ℝⁿˣⁿ cannot be even close to identity, because its rank
  > is at most m, making ‖WᵀW − I‖²_F a biased minimization objective."
  另 §2（第 3 頁）："WᵀW = I may never happen for an overcomplete 'fat' W due to rank
  deficiency of its gram matrix".
- **座標慣例要注意**：Bansal 的 W ∈ ℝ^{m×n}，**m = S×H×C（= 我們的 d）、n = M（= C_out）**
  （§3 開頭："we will first reshape C into a matrix form W′ ∈ ℝ^{m′×n′}, where
  m′ = S × H × C and n′ = M"）。也就是 Bansal 的 W 是 main.tex 的 Wᵀ。
  「overcomplete」= m < n = **C_out > d**，這點與 main.tex 一致；秩虧損的 Gram 是
  **濾波器之間的 Gram**（Bansal 的 WᵀW，在 main.tex 慣例下是 WWᵀ ∈ ℝ^{C_out×C_out}）。

**main.tex 第 259–260 行的用語問題（要改）**
> "Orthogonality regularisers penalise the distance of a layer's kernel matrix
> W ∈ ℝ^{C_out×d} ... from an isometry; when C_out > d no isometry exists"

照字面這句是錯的：C_out×d 且 C_out > d 的矩陣**可以**是等距（WᵀW = I_d，行正交），
不存在的是**列正交／濾波器互相正交**（WWᵀ = I_{C_out} 不可能，秩 ≤ d）。Bansal 講的
正是後者。建議改成 "when C_out > d the C_out filters cannot be mutually orthogonal
(the filter Gram WWᵀ has rank at most d)" 或直接講「無法有正交列」。

- §3.4 Unified Row and Column Orthogonalization（第 6431 頁）：
  > "In previous sections, we assume n ≤ d, and obtain an orthogonalization solution. One
  > question that remains is how to handle the situation when n > d. When n > d, the rows
  > of W cannot be orthogonal, because the rank of W is less than/equal to d."
- §3.5 Theorem 1（第 6432 頁）：
  > "Theorem 1. Let ĥ = Wx, where WWᵀ = I and W ∈ ℝ^{n×d}. Assume: (1) E_x(x) = 0,
  > cov(x) = σ₁²I, and (2) E(∂L/∂ĥ) = 0, cov(∂L/∂ĥ) = σ₂²I. If n = d, we have the following
  > properties: (1) ‖ĥ‖ = ‖x‖; (2) E(ĥ) = 0, cov(ĥ) = σ₁²I; (3) ‖∂L/∂x‖ = ‖∂L/∂ĥ‖;

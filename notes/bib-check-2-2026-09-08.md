is removed. The `% UNVERIFIED:` comment lines that follow six entries in the

## Search limitations

- **DBLP** (dblp.org and the dagstuhl mirror, API and HTML) is behind an Anubis
  bot-check and returned no data from either curl or WebFetch. Consequences:
  page numbers for NeurIPS papers before 2020 (`lu2017expressive`,
  `jacot2018ntk`, `bansal2018orthogonality`, `veit2016ensembles`) could not be
  confirmed from a primary or DBLP source; the NeurIPS proceedings `.bib`
  files carry empty `pages` fields for those years. OpenAlex reports
  550–558 (Veit) and 6232–6240 (Lu); these were **not** inserted.
- **dl.acm.org** returns HTTP 403 to both curl and WebFetch, so the ACM article
  number for `waleffe2023pcn` is unknown (Crossref gives only "1–27").
- **Semantic Scholar** API answered 429 (rate-limited) and was not retried.
- **OpenReview** `notes` endpoint requires a browser challenge; only the
  `notes/search` endpoint worked, and it rate-limited after ~10 calls, so a
  few lookups were repeated with long pauses. All ICLR/TMLR/workshop
  confirmations above come from that endpoint's own bibtex records.
- **MDPI** page for `hanin2019universal` was not readable (403/JS), but the
  arXiv record of 1708.02691 carries the journal reference and DOI, which
  settles the relation.
- **Google Books** search-inside for Horn & Johnson returns no snippets, and no
  accessible citing paper attaches a result to "Theorem 4.3.28"; that theorem
  number remains unverified. Theorem 4.5.9 = Ostrowski is confirmed only
  through a citing paper, not the book itself.
- **PMLR** has no AISTATS 2026 volume yet, so `schulte2026rethinking` has no
  volume/pages.
- Not checked: anything outside the 39 entries above; content claims inside
  annotes (theorem statements, figure numbers) other than the two explicitly
  flagged items (Horn & Johnson theorem numbers, Wightman workshop award).
